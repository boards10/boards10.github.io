Focus areas: where to spend your time

  • Maths: Algebra and Geometry are 35 of 80 marks. Practise quadratic equations, arithmetic progression, triangles and coordinate geometry first, then trigonometry and statistics.
  • Science: Chemical Substances and World of Living are 50 of 80 marks. Prioritise carbon and its compounds, metals and non-metals, life processes, control and coordination, heredity.
  • Physics numericals: electricity and light add 25 marks together. Learn the formulas and the ray diagrams.
  • SST: every part is 20 marks, so a weak Economics or Polity section costs as much as a weak History one. Practise map work.
  • English and Hindi: do the writing tasks in the PYQs, such as analytical paragraph, and Hindi grammar and vigyapan lekhan.

How to use Class 10 PYQs

  1. Finish a chapter from the NCERT textbook and its exercises.
  2. Solve that chapter's questions from 5 years of PYQs.
  3. Mark the questions that repeat. Those are your must-know topics.
  4. Two months before boards, solve full PYQs in 3 hours.
  5. Finish with the CBSE sample paper for Class 10 2026-27 and compare with the marking scheme line by line.

Pair PYQs with NCERT solutions for Class 10 and the NCERT exemplar for harder questions. Higher-level book exercises are optional, so do them only after you finish NCERT.
