Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Boards10 (accessible at https://boards10.github.io/). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected while you visit our educational archive.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user registration, login, or personal information (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers) to access our question papers and study resources. We may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Umami Analytics) to understand aggregated traffic patterns, page views, and general browser/device types to improve our platform performance.

3. Cookies and Tracking

Boards10 does not use intrusive advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Basic browser session data or minimal analytics cookies may be utilized solely to measure site performance and visitor numbers anonymously.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external resources, GitHub releases, educational repositories, and social sharing platforms (WhatsApp, Telegram, Reddit). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website is designed for students preparing for Class 10 board examinations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub Repository.
