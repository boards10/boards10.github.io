How to Improve Your Marks in CBSE Class 10

Improving marks isn't just about studying more—it's about studying smarter. Here are the key habits that top-scoring students adopt.

1. Master the Marking Scheme

The biggest mistake is ignoring how points are allocated. Every CBSE subject has a specific marking scheme. Look at past papers to see that a 5-mark question requires 5 distinct points, not a long, rambling paragraph.

2. The "3-Hour" Rule

You must practice sitting for 3 hours straight. This builds stamina. When you simulate the exam environment (no phone, no music, no breaks), you learn how to pace your writing and handle the pressure of tricky questions.

3. Active Recall over Passive Reading

Don't just re-read your notes. Cover them and try to explain the concept out loud. If you can't explain it, you haven't mastered it. Active recall is the fastest way to move information from short-term to long-term memory.

4. Presentation Matters

Use headings, sub-headings, and bullet points. In Science, always draw a diagram if the question asks for it, or even if it doesn't, if it helps clarify your answer. Label your diagrams clearly. Neat presentation makes it easier for the examiner to give you full marks.
