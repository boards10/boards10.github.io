CBSE Class 10 English previous year question papers

English is scored out of 80 in the board paper plus 20 internal marks. The paper tests reading, writing, grammar, and literature from the prescribed textbook. Solving previous year papers shows the length of unseen passages, the letter and analytical-paragraph formats, and how literature questions are worded.

Open the English PDF viewer

What the paper usually contains

  • Reading: Unseen passages. Answers should use words from the passage and not add facts that are not there.
  • Writing: A formal letter, often a letter to the editor or a complaint or order letter, and a short analytical paragraph based on a chart, cue, or situation.
  • Grammar: Gap filling, editing, reported speech, tenses, determiners, and subject-verb agreement. These are short, high-accuracy marks.
  • Literature: Extracts plus short and long answers. Language and Literature uses First Flight and Footprints Without Feet. Communicative English uses a different set, so match the paper to the course you study.

How to use these papers

  1. Read one full paper without writing, and mark every question type.
  2. Write one timed paper in three hours. Leave ten minutes to check spelling and format.
  3. For literature, answer in points and quote one short line from the text when you can.
  4. Keep a one-page list of letter format, analytical paragraph structure, and grammar rules you miss.
  5. Compare your answers with the official marking scheme when CBSE publishes it.
