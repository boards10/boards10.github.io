Standard vs. Basic Mathematics in CBSE Class 10

CBSE offers two levels of Mathematics exams:

  • Mathematics Standard: Designed for students who wish to pursue Mathematics in Class 11 and 12 (Science/Commerce streams). Tests higher-order problem-solving and conceptual depth.
  • Mathematics Basic: Easier question paper tailored for students who do not wish to pursue higher-level mathematics. Focuses on foundational application and standard textbook problems.

High-Weightage Chapters for Class 10 Maths

  • Triangles & Circles: Theorem proofs (Thales Theorem, Tangent properties) carry 6-8 marks.
  • Introduction to Trigonometry & Applications: Height and distance word problems are guaranteed 5-mark questions.
  • Statistics & Probability: Mean, Median, Mode computations offer direct scoring opportunities.
  • Quadratic Equations & Arithmetic Progressions: Word problems based on speed, distance, and daily life applications.
