📋 Exam Pattern, Format & Blueprint

What is the exact exam pattern and section-wise breakdown for CBSE Class 10?

The standard theory paper is for 80 Marks with a duration of 3 Hours. The remaining 20 Marks are allocated to internal assessment by your school.

Standard 5-Section Structure (Science & Mathematics):
  • Section A: 20 objective questions (1 mark each) comprising 16 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) and 4 Assertion-Reason questions. (Total: 20 marks)
  • Section B: 6 Very Short Answer (VSA) questions (2 marks each). (Total: 12 marks)
  • Section C: 7 Short Answer (SA) questions (3 marks each). (Total: 21 marks)
  • Section D: 3 Long Answer (LA) questions (5 marks each). (Total: 15 marks)
  • Section E: 3 Case-based / Passage-based / Integrated units of assessment (4 marks each with sub-parts). (Total: 12 marks)

Internal choices are provided in approximately 33% of questions across sections.

What are Competency-Based Questions and how do I prepare for them?

Competency-Based Questions (CBQs) assess conceptual understanding and real-world application rather than memorization. As per CBSE guidelines, CBQs account for 50% of total marks.

  • Case-Based Studies: A paragraph, data table, graph, or practical scenario followed by 2–3 analytical questions.
  • Assertion and Reason: Two statements where you determine the truth of each and whether the Reason correctly explains the Assertion.
  • Daily-Life Applications: Explaining physical phenomena (e.g., dispersion of light in raindrops, extraction of metals, baking powder chemistry).

Pro Tip: Read NCERT textbook boxes ("Do You Know?", "Activity", and exemplar problems) as most case studies originate from these sections.

What is the difference between CBSE Class 10 Standard and Basic Mathematics?

CBSE introduced two difficulty tiers for Mathematics in Class 10 to reduce student stress:

  • Mathematics Standard (Code 041): Intended for students who intend to opt for Mathematics in Class 11 and 12 (Science or Commerce with Math). It involves higher-order thinking skills (HOTS), non-routine problems, and conceptual proofs.
  • Mathematics Basic (Code 241): Designed for students who do not plan on pursuing Mathematics beyond Class 10. The paper primarily tests direct NCERT examples, textbook exercise questions, and fundamental formulas.

Both exams follow the same syllabus and NCERT textbook.

💡 Subject-Wise Preparation Strategy & High-Weightage Chapters

How should I prepare for Class 10 Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)?
  • Physics: Focus heavily on Ray Diagrams (Convex/Concave mirrors and lenses), lens & mirror formula numerical problems, refractive index, and Ohm’s Law circuits (series & parallel resistance). Practice drawing neat, labeled diagrams with proper ray direction arrows.
  • Chemistry: Memorize balanced chemical equations with color changes, precipitate names, and gas evolution observations. Master the reactions in "Acids, Bases & Salts" and "Carbon & Its Compounds" (homologous series, saponification, esterification).
  • Biology: Practice labeled diagrams for Human Digestive System, Heart, Nephron, Human Brain, and Female Reproductive System. Be thorough with Mendel’s dihybrid & monohybrid crosses.
How should I prepare for Class 10 Mathematics to score 90+?
  1. Solve every single NCERT exercise problem and solved example twice.
  2. Pay special attention to Theorems (Basic Proportionality Theorem / Thales Theorem).
  3. Master high-weightage chapters: Triangles, Trigonometry, Statistics, Surface Areas and Volumes, and Quadratic Equations.
  4. Never skip writing units (cm², m³, etc.) and final statement answers.
  5. Solve at least 5 complete sample papers under timed 3-hour conditions using our archive.
How can I score full marks in Class 10 Social Science (SST)?

Social Science requires structured, point-wise presentation:

  • History: Create chronological timelines for "The Rise of Nationalism in Europe" and "Nationalism in India". Write answers with sub-headings.
  • Geography: Practice map work diligently (dams, iron ore mines, nuclear plants, airports, and major ports). Map pointing carries guaranteed marks.
  • Political Science & Economics: Understand definitions clearly (Power Sharing, Federalism, GDP, Sectors of the Economy, Self Help Groups). Provide real-world Indian examples.
